What the data showed

Return on ad spend by month

Google peaked at 16.2 in April and fell to 3.4 by August. Facebook never passed 2.5.

Monthly ad spend and ad revenue

Spend rose 2.7 times in June, but return fell from 2.72 in May to 1.59 by August.

What I recommended

  • Google returned 5.86 per rupee against Facebook's 1.89, yet Facebook held 93.67% of spend. Shift budget toward what works.
  • The 25% target cannot be met by moving budget alone. I said so in the report and listed what else is needed: a checkout and tracking fix, order value recovery, and a creative and offer shift.
